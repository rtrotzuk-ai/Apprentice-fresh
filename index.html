One quick step on iPhone

Apple only allows alerts from sites saved to your Home Screen.

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari (the square with the arrow).
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Apprentice from your Home Screen and tap Turn on alerts.

Not in Safari? Open this page in Safari first — other iPhone browsers can't add it.

This browser can't receive alerts

Try Chrome, Edge or Firefox on Android or desktop, or Safari on iPhone (iOS 16.4 or newer, saved to the Home Screen).
